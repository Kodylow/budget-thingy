## Budget Monitor: your practical walkthrough

- For Comcast members and team administrators
- Find your spending and current projects
- Understand funding, monthly Agent limits, and the actions your role permits

## Sign in and understand your access

- Choose Log in; sign-in continues in the current browser tab
- Members can review their own spending
- Administrators see additional information only within their authorized scope
- Management tools appear only when your permissions allow them

## Start with Home

- Review your personal spending summary and trends
- Use My Projects to see your current project inventory
- Follow linked details to investigate an amount
- Home is not automatically an account-wide spending view

## Choose the right reporting period

- Full period is the default reporting range
- Start and end dates are inclusive
- The contract runs from May 20, 2026 to May 20, 2027
- Reporting dates do not change the billing cycle or monthly Agent limit
- A forecast is an estimate, not additional recorded spending

## Find your projects—including those without spend

- Open My Projects from the navigation
- Search, sort, and page through your current self-owned projects
- Published describes current deployment status
- Projects without spending still belong in the inventory
- Reporting dates change spending amounts, not catalog membership

## Investigate spending without double-counting

- Open Spend and choose an available detail view
- Narrow the authorized scope with search and filters
- Open a group or member to investigate supporting detail
- Project-attributed amounts explain activity; do not add them to workspace or member totals

## Team admins: review your assigned scope

- Open My Team to review the people and groups available to you
- Investigate changes before changing funding or limits
- Check the selected scope and dates when comparing amounts
- A shared funding team does not grant access to otherwise restricted spending

## Funding and Agent limits are different

- Budget allocations are planning funding used for monitoring
- Monthly Agent limits are platform controls that may block paid Agent usage
- Changing a planning allocation does not automatically change an Agent limit
- Remaining monthly limit uses the current billing cycle, not a short report range

## Change Agent limits safely—when authorized

- Open Management → Limits
- Select eligible members in an authorized workspace and enter the amount
- Review the targets before confirming the change
- Check which results are verified, pending, failed, or unknown
- An unconfirmed outcome is not a successful limit change

## Manage planning funding—when authorized

- Open Management → Budget allocations
- Review opening funding and dated monthly additions separately
- Review and confirm an opening-funding change or monthly addition
- Approved adjustments remain additive
- These actions manage local planning funding, not platform limits

## Use additional reporting and admin tools

- Custom Reports is available only to authorized roles
- Choose permitted funding teams and the reporting dates you need
- Annual remaining belongs to the budget period, not a short selected range
- Email activity lets authorized administrators review alert activity
- Org Insights requires account-usage permission

## When a number or access check looks wrong

- Check your scope and reporting dates first
- Unavailable means unknown—not zero
- Use Reconnect after a temporary access-check failure
- Administrators can open Account menu → Data quality for detailed notes
- Use Help for the walkthrough; contact your administrator for missing permissions
